Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information Badr - The Crossing ("the game") handles, why, and who it is shared with. The game is made by DevLab Venture Studio. Questions: contact us through devlabtechnologies.com.

What we do not collect

The game has no accounts, no advertising and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Information the game handles

  • Anonymous player ID. To play online, the game signs in anonymously with Unity Authentication, which creates a random ID for your installation. It is used only to connect you to matches.
  • Connection data. Online matches connect through Unity's Relay and Lobby services, which process your IP address and match data to route game traffic.
  • Voice chat. If you turn on voice chat and allow microphone access, your voice is sent live to your teammates through Vivox (a Unity service) while you hold the talk button. We do not record or store voice.
  • Text chat. Messages you type are sent to the players in your match. We do not store them after the match.
  • Local network. For matches on the same Wi-Fi, the game looks for nearby games on your local network. This stays on your network.
  • Settings. Your language, controls and other preferences are saved only on your device.

Who processes it

Unity Technologies operates the Authentication, Relay, Lobby and Vivox services on our behalf. See Unity's privacy policy. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else.

Retention and deletion

Voice and text chat are not kept. The anonymous player ID is kept by Unity while you use online play; deleting the game removes it from your device. To ask for deletion of any data linked to your player ID, contact us through devlabtechnologies.com.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
